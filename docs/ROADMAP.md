# Direction, mood and progression

Updated: 2026-10-14. This document distinguishes goals from implemented behavior.

The project began with a humorous two-stage slipper family and grew into distinctive attack rhythms plus movement. Its suggested mood is playful, agile and mechanically satisfying: comedy in the object/idea, precision in use. Saber emphasizes sharp acceleration, Piercer a deliberate bolt rhythm, Crossbow a short burst and Glider flowing momentum.

Visual direction: recognizable Terraria-style silhouettes, tangible wood/leather/metal, restrained colors, readable impacts and short trails. Effects should communicate direction and timing rather than obscure action. This is a creative direction derived from current choices, not a finalized art guide.

## Next priorities

1. Verify and tune existing feel in actual terrain/combat: Saber charge/dash, Glider turns/boost/facing, reload rhythms.
2. Inspect full-roll character/armor/weapon alignment and improve temporary wing art.
3. Balance acquisition, costs and damage using real encounters; retain fixed code tuning rather than reopening every value as a debug config slider.
4. Validate vanilla accessory interactions and real multiplayer before widening content.
5. Add Saber/Bolt Action/Lancer accessories and weapons that create different charge, mobility, reload, penetration or momentum/spacing playstyles. No exact accessory list is approved yet.

Ask of each new item: what does it make the player do differently? Avoid adding only a larger damage number.
