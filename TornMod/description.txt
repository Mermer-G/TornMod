Six experimental weapons: Mom's Slipper, Slipperon, Saber, Piercer, Repeating Crossbow and Iron Lance.
Glider Wings is a momentum-based movement accessory: hold Jump to glide and Down to dive.
Saber and Iron Lance use melee damage; Piercer and Repeating Crossbow use ranged damage.
Gameplay Settings includes optional Instant Respawn, Invincibility and Warp to Cursor. Weapon balance is fixed.
